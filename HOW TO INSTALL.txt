SIM RACING COACH (BETA) - HOW TO INSTALL AND USE
================================================

Works with iRacing, Assetto Corsa Competizione, Assetto Corsa,
Assetto Corsa EVO and Automobilista 2, on Windows. Everything stays on
your PC: no account, nothing uploaded, works offline.

AUTOMOBILISTA 2 ONLY (once): in the game go to Options > System >
Shared Memory and choose "Project CARS 2". The other sims need no setting.

1. GET THE RECORDER (once)
   a. Copy "SimLapRecorder.exe" somewhere easy, e.g. your Desktop.
      It needs nothing else installed.
   b. The first time you double-click it, Windows may show a blue box
      "Windows protected your PC". That's normal for small programs that
      aren't from a big publisher: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
      Windows remembers, so you only do this once.
   c. If your antivirus warns about it, that's a known false alarm with
      programs packaged this way (PyInstaller).

2. EACH TIME YOU RACE
   a. Double-click "SimLapRecorder.exe". A small dark window appears.
      Only one copy runs at a time; starting it again brings the window
      to the front.
   b. Start your sim as usual. You can start the recorder before or after
      the sim - order doesn't matter.
   c. Drive. The dot turns red and says "Recording". Each lap appears in
      the list when you cross the line. Your fastest valid lap is purple.
   d. Close the window when you're done.

3. LOOK AT YOUR LAPS (THE ANALYSER)
   Click "Analyse last session" in the recorder window. After a second or
   two your web browser opens the analyser with that session loaded:
   your laps, your ideal lap (your best sections stitched together), the
   track map, where the time went corner by corner, the other drivers,
   and setup suggestions fitted to what your car can adjust.
   "Analyse another..." lets you pick any older session.
   The page is saved as analysis.html in the session folder, so you can
   open it again later by double-clicking it. It's big (10-30 MB) because
   every lap is inside it. You can do this while you're still driving.

4. VOICE COACH (optional)
   Tick "Voice coach" in the recorder window and it talks to you while
   you drive, through your PC's default sound device:
     - after your first clean lap: "Reference lap set"
     - on the straight after a corner where you lost a tenth or more
       against your best lap, one short note with the reason, e.g.
       "Les Combes, two tenths down. You coasted. Ease off the brake
       gradually, all the way to the apex."
     - "best yet" when you beat your best version of a corner
     - your lap time at the line, against your best
   It never talks in a corner or a braking zone, waits 5 seconds between
   notes, says at most 5 corner notes a lap, and after a spin says "big
   moment" once and stays quiet until the line. "Test voice" plays a
   sample. The Volume box (-6 to +9 dB) sets voice and beep volume.

5. BRAKE BEEPS (optional)
   Tick "Brake beeps" and you hear a countdown before each braking
   zone: two short pips and a third, higher one. Press the brake on the
   third pip. "Test beep" plays it. The braking points come from your
   own best lap of the session (from the lap after your first clean
   one). They work with the voice coach on or off.

6. ORIGINAL ASSETTO CORSA: OTHER CARS (optional)
   AC only shares your own car, so to compare with the AI or other
   drivers it needs a small in-game app, SimLapOthers, included in the
   "assettocorsa" folder of this download.
     a. Open your Assetto Corsa folder: in Steam, right-click Assetto
        Corsa > Manage > Browse local files.
     b. Copy the "apps" and "content" folders from this download's
        "assettocorsa" folder into it. If Windows asks, choose to merge
        the folders (nothing of yours is replaced).
     c. Content Manager: Settings > Assetto Corsa > Apps (Python apps):
        tick SimLapOthers. Without Content Manager: in the game's main
        menu, Options > General > UI Modules, tick SimLapOthers.
   The app shares the other cars even if you never open its window.
   Your own laps are recorded with or without it.
   If you own original AC, the recorder also reads its track files for
   corner names, and its car files for each car's ideal tyre pressure and
   temperature range. That happens on your PC only.

WHERE THE LAPS GO
   Documents\SimLapRecorder\ - one folder per session, named with the
   date, sim, track and car. "Open recordings folder" jumps there.
   Each folder holds lap_001.csv, lap_002.csv ... plus session.json.
   iRacing sessions also save your car setup (setup_xxxx.yaml).
   If other cars were on track, others.csv.gz and drivers.json hold
   their positions so you can compare against them in the analyser.

WHAT THE LAP STATUSES MEAN
   Valid                  - a clean full lap, ready for analysis
   Invalid (track limits) - full lap, but the sim flagged it
   Pit / out lap          - included the pit lane
   Incomplete             - joined mid-lap, reset, or teleported

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
   Please report it with the sim, car and track, plus these files from
   Documents\SimLapRecorder: recorder.log (and crash.txt if there is
   one), and the session folder, zipped.
